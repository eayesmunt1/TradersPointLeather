Returns and Refunds

14-day returns on Ready to Ship pieces without initials, if undamaged.

What can be returned

A Ready to Ship piece without initials can be returned within 14 days of delivery, as long as it is undamaged. You pay the return shipping.

Final sale

These are made for you, so they are final sale unless they arrive defective or damaged:

  • Made to Order wallets and designer builds
  • Any piece with initials
  • Belts and dog collars made to your length or size

Defective or damaged

If anything arrives defective or damaged, email customer-support@traderspointleather.com with a photo and we will make it right.

How to return

Email customer-support@traderspointleather.com with your order details before sending anything back, and we will reply with the return address.

Refunds

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving. Shipping was free, so there is no shipping charge to refund.
